During my college years, I've had the opportunity to work on a good amount of games either through projects or game jams. I've won several award including project CASSIOPEE best project award (against over 80 submissions) and the 2024 Unijams publics choice award for Irma Rnak. 

Irma Rnak is an ambitious game featuring voice acting and subtitle where you play as a clairvoyant reading your customers future. ## Computer Graphics
Computer graphics is a sub-field of computer science which studies methods of digitally synthesizing and manipulating visual content. Imagine you want to render a teapot on screen : you have the construct the geometry, define the material and calculate how light bounces from the surface. Computer Graphics is all that and much more.

During my time studying computer graphics, I've had the opportunity to implement various research papers on the subject. Including ["Real time polygonal-light shading with linearly transformed cosines"](https://eheitzresearch.wordpress.com/415-2/) which is a foundationnal research paper especially for game engines. The paper showcases a way of creating polygonal light sources : for example a rectangular computer screen lighting up a dark room or a circle headlight on a car. Up until then most engines used point-based lighting which lacks the complexity of actual lighting.

You can find my breakdown and implementation of the paper in WebGPU [here](https://github.com/Jeremy-afk/image-synthesis/tree/main).
