# Highlights
## Game Developpement
Game development has been a major part of my journey as a software engineer. Throughout my studies, I participated in numerous game projects and game jams, earning several distinctions, including the Project Cassiopee Best Project Award among more than 80 submissions and the 2024 Unijam Public Choice Award for Irma Rnak.

[Irma Rnak](https://soldatspectre76.itch.io/irma-rnak) is a narrative-driven game created in 48 hours. You play as a fraudulent clairvoyant who must infer what clients want to hear by observing contextual clues. Success leads to glowing reviews; failure quickly exposes your deception. Despite the tight deadline, the project features full voice acting and subtitles.

Beyond development, I served as president of a game design club, where my team and I organized weekly workshops and lectures to introduce freshmen to game design. I also founded a game jam that has continued to run annually since 2024.

Most of my games are available on [itch.io](https://pumpkin-moon-studio.itch.io/). Feel free to check it out !

## Computer Graphics
Computer graphics focuses on the generation and simulation of visual content. From geometric modeling and material representation to realistic lighting and rendering, it combines mathematics, physics, and software engineering to create interactive visual experiences.

During my studies, I implemented and explored several influential graphics research papers. One project focused on [Real-Time Polygonal-Light Shading with Linearly Transformed Cosines](https://eheitzresearch.wordpress.com/415-2/) by Eric Heitz and colleagues, a widely adopted technique for efficiently rendering area lights in real time. The method enables realistic lighting from complex light sources such as monitors, windows, or vehicle headlights while remaining suitable for interactive applications and game engines.

You can find my breakdown and implementation of the paper in WebGPU [here](https://github.com/Jeremy-afk/image-synthesis/tree/main).
