# Areas of Interest
## Game Developpement
Game development has been a major part of my journey as a software engineer. Throughout my studies, I participated in numerous game projects and game jams, earning several distinctions, including the Project Cassiopee Best Project Award among more than 80 submissions and the 2024 Unijam Public Choice Award for [Irma Rnak](#irma-rnak).

Beyond development, I served as president of a game design club, where my team and I organized weekly workshops and lectures to introduce freshmen to game design. I also founded a game jam that has continued to run annually since 2024.

Most of my games are available on [itch.io](https://pumpkin-moon-studio.itch.io/). Feel free to check it out !

## Computer Graphics
During my studies, I explored rendering, lighting, and physically-based shading through both coursework and personal projects.

I particularly enjoy implementing research papers and understanding how modern rendering techniques are translated into real-time applications.

One project involved implementing Eric Heitz's [Real-Time Polygonal-Light Shading with Linearly Transformed Cosines](https://eheitzresearch.wordpress.com/415-2/) in WebGPU, exploring efficient area-light rendering techniques used in modern game engines.

You can find the implementation and technical breakdown [here](https://github.com/Jeremy-afk/image-synthesis/tree/main).

# Projects
## Irma Rnak
[Irma Rnak](https://soldatspectre76.itch.io/irma-rnak) is a narrative-driven game created in 48 hours. You play as a fraudulent clairvoyant who must infer what clients want to hear by observing contextual clues. Success leads to glowing reviews; failure quickly exposes your deception. Despite the tight deadline, the project features full voice acting and subtitles.
